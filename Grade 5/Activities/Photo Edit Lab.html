How to check a photo before you believe it
  • Does anything look odd? Look at sizes, shadows, edges and light.
  • Who posted it first? Is it a name you know and trust?
  • Do trusted news websites show the same photo?
  • Ask a grown-up to do a reverse image search. It finds where a photo first appeared.
