Sort It Board: Joke, Conflict or Bullying?

Ask three questions: Is it on purpose to hurt? Does it happen again and again? Is one person much stronger or are many ganging up? If the answer is yes to these, it may be bullying. A one-time disagreement is conflict. A joke is when everyone is laughing, including the person it is about.

Tap a card, then tap the column where it belongs. When all cards are placed, tap Check my board.
